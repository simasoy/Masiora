01 — The Studio

Design rooted in material, light and the rhythm of daily life.

We believe a well-designed space should feel effortless. Our work balances warmth and restraint: textured plaster, oiled timber, linen and stone, layered with pieces that carry meaning.

Every project begins with listening. We take the time to understand how you move through your home, what you love, and what you need — then we design spaces that support it, beautifully and for the long term.

04 — Process

From first conversation to final cushion

  1. Discover

    We meet in your space, talk through how you live, your budget and timeline, and gather the inspiration that matters to you.

  2. Concept

    We present a design direction — layouts, mood, material palette and key pieces — and refine it together until it feels right.

  3. Develop

    Detailed drawings, specifications and sourcing. We coordinate with trades and suppliers so nothing is left to chance.

  4. Deliver

    We oversee installation and style every detail, handing over a finished space ready to be lived in.
