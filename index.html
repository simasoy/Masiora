The Studio

We design homes that feel like stepping outside on a clear morning: calm, fresh and quietly dramatic. Raw timber, limewash, linen and stone, in soft mineral colours drawn from mountain and sea.

Rustic  ·  Dramatic  ·  Earthy  ·  Tranquil  ·  Fresh  ·  Organic

Drawn from nature, made for living.

Every project begins with listening. We take the time to understand how you move through your home, what you love and what you need, then design spaces that support it, beautifully and for the long term.

We favour materials that age gracefully and pieces that carry meaning, layered with restraint so each room can breathe.

Process

From first conversation to final detail

  1. I

    Discover

    We meet in your space and talk through how you live, your budget and timeline, and the inspiration that matters to you.

  2. II

    Concept

    A design direction (layouts, mood, material palette and key pieces) refined together until it feels right.

  3. III

    Develop

    Detailed drawings, specifications and sourcing, coordinated with trades and suppliers so nothing is left to chance.

  4. IV

    Deliver

    We oversee installation and style every detail, handing over a finished space ready to be lived in.
