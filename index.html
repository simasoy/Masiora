01 — The Studio

Drawn from nature: weathered timber, still water, mountain stone.

Rustic · Dramatic · Earthy · Tranquil · Fresh · Organic

We believe a home should feel like stepping outside on a clear morning: calm, fresh and quietly dramatic. Our work layers raw wood, limewash, linen and stone with soft mineral colours taken from the landscape.

Every project begins with listening. We take the time to understand how you move through your home, what you love and what you need, then design spaces that support it, beautifully and for the long term.

04 — Process

From first conversation to final cushion

  1. Discover

    We meet in your space and talk through how you live, your budget and timeline, and the inspiration that matters to you.

  2. Concept

    We present a design direction (layouts, mood, material palette and key pieces) and refine it together until it feels right.

  3. Develop

    Detailed drawings, specifications and sourcing. We coordinate with trades and suppliers so nothing is left to chance.

  4. Deliver

    We oversee installation and style every detail, handing over a finished space ready to be lived in.
